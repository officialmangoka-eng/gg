## How pricing works

Your subscription is the key to everything. Once it is active, every core feature is available to you with no limits on how often you use it.

A small set of add-on services runs on credits, so you only pay for the extra volume you actually send. You can manage your plan and top up credits at any time from [Billing](https://wapimax.com/dashboard/billing/plans).
