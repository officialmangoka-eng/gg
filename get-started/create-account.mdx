## Set up your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to the [registration page](https://wapimax.com/register) and fill in your details. When you submit the form, we take you straight to the login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Sign in with the email and password you just created. Because your account is new, we send you to the plans page next.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Look over the available plans and pick the one that suits you. Once your payment goes through, your account is marked as **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  That is all there is to it. With an active account, every WapiMax product is open to you.
</Check>

Before you make your first request, you will need an API key. The [Authorization](/get-started/authorization) guide shows you how to create one.
