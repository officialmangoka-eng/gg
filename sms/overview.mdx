## Base URL

```text
https://api.wapimax.com/sms/v1
```

## How to check current free sender IDs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your dashboard">
    Sign in to your WapiMax account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to SMS">
    In the sidebar, under **Communication**, select **SMS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Sender IDs">
    Select **Sender ID**. All available sender IDs are listed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the sender ID exactly as listed, e.g. `TextSMS`, in the `sender_id` field.
</Tip>

## Before you send

- An active WapiMax account
- A plan that includes SMS
- An active sender ID
- SMS wallet balance

<Warning>
  Most sender IDs support Kenyan numbers only. Confirm your sender ID supports international SMS before sending outside Kenya.
</Warning>
