title: "Register Webhook"
description: "Register webhook endpoints from the WapiMax dashboard to receive real-time WhatsApp events."
---

<Warning>
  Webhooks are registered from the WapiMax dashboard.
</Warning>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in at [wapimax.com/login](https://wapimax.com/login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Webhooks">
    Go to [Developer > Webhooks](https://wapimax.com/dashboard/developer/webhooks).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your endpoint">
    Create a webhook and enter your HTTPS URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select events">
    Choose events, or `all`. See [Events](/whatsapp/webhooks/webhook-payload#events).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the configuration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requirements

- Public HTTPS URL
- Accepts POST requests
- Returns a 2xx response
